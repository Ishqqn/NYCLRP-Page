GETTING STARTED

How to join

Three steps between you and your first shift on the streets of Liberty City.

STEP 1

Join the Discord

Our Discord is where applications, announcements and rank changes are posted first. Tap "Join our Discord" above.

STEP 2

Verify & read the rules

Link your Roblox account and read the server rulebook in the welcome channel before you queue.

STEP 3

Join the server

Use the "Join the Roblox server" button, pick a department, and you're on duty.
